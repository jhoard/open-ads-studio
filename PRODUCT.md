# Open Ads Studio

## Register

product

## Users

Solo brand owners creating product advertising on a laptop or desktop. They review a brand kit, protect product labels, select a recipe, approve creative outputs and export disclosed assets. Context: a focused editing session in ordinary indoor lighting, with a dark preview workspace that keeps attention on the creative.

## Product Purpose

Turn a product photo and brand URL into on-brand ad stills and video with audio using open-weight ComfyUI workflows. Preserve packaging identity, distinguish demo outputs from live generation, and make review and export decisions understandable.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, capable. The studio has a neutral identity; the customer's colors and display typography belong inside brand previews and creatives.

## Anti-references

Avoid an oversized brand poster crowding a narrow form, faint field boundaries, ambiguous color-only selections, unnecessary technical jargon, and decorative motion that delays a task. Do not turn the editor into a marketing landing page.

## Design Principles

- Keep the next action visible and explain prerequisites for unavailable steps.
- Group related decisions and keep editing and preview space balanced.
- Use familiar form controls, consistent navigation and explicit state labels.
- Preserve the existing product workflow and generation safeguards.
- Treat brand previews as content, not the interface theme.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

User-confirmed direction: target WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard access and reduced motion. Include readable text and control contrast, visible focus, meaningful labels, non-color selection indicators, sensible focus after navigation, responsive reflow, and status/error announcements. Verification should report tested scope without claiming full conformance from automated checks alone.
