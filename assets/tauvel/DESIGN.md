# Tauvel: brand kit (cached DESIGN.md for the demo)

Source: Tauvel design directions (prompts.json, Jul 2026), updated for the unisex line (Him/Her dropped Jul 31 2026).

## Product
- Dark chocolate infused with coffee, with creatine. One unisex bar.
- Label facts: CREATINE 5G, NET WT 60G.
- Category: Food & beverage / supplements. Risk rating: medium (see docs/brand-safety-ontology.md).

## Positioning and tone
- Tagline: "Strength, crafted in chocolate."
- Luxury craft chocolate first, performance second. Calm, editorial, premium. Never gym-bro, never neon.
- Audience: adults with a daily ritual (coffee, training, focused work) who buy premium food.

## Palette
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| espresso | #2B1A12 | primary wrapper, dark grounds |
| cacao | #4A2E21 | secondary surfaces, shadows |
| brushed-gold | #B8925A | foil, monogram, accents |
| cream | #F2EADF | light grounds, type on dark |
| charcoal | #1E1E1E | type on light |
| stone | #CFC6BA | props, surfaces |

## Typography
- Wordmark and display: high-contrast Didone serif (Didot / Bodoni family), uppercase, wide letterspacing.
- Supporting: small letterspaced sans caps.

## Marks
- Wordmark: TAUVEL.
- Monogram: a single refined "T" inside a thin circle, brushed gold. Embossed on every chocolate square.

## Packaging
- Rigid folded paperboard sleeve, soft-touch matte espresso, over a brushed-gold foil inner wrap.
- Fine topographic contour lines blind-embossed tone-on-tone across the sleeve.
- Gold hot-foil wordmark and monogram on the front.

## Scene language
- Warm natural stone, travertine, linen, ceramic espresso cups, whole roasted coffee beans, cacao nibs.
- Soft directional daylight, long gentle shadows, shallow depth of field.
